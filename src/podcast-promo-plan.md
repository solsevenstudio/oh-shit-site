# The Graft Podcast, Round 2 — promo plan

You're recording Episode 2 tomorrow (chemotherapy + what's next / the Isle of Wight). Here's a four-phase campaign built around it, with drafted posts you can adapt. The whole thing also doubles as "organic marketing for Part Two" — so even if someone never listens to the episode, they hear about the book.

---

## Ask the host for 4 things — today

Send the podcast team a short note before you record. This is the single highest-leverage thing you'll do:

1. **Release date** — so you can pre-schedule every post.
2. **A couple of short clips** (30–60 seconds each, vertical 9:16 with captions burned in). Most podcasts cut 2–3 of these per episode now. If they don't, offer to do it yourself from the raw audio/video.
3. **The video feed**, if it's a video podcast — so you can lift your own thumbnails and stills.
4. **Tags and handles** of anyone the host wants tagged, plus the right hashtag for the podcast.

Tell them you'll cross-promote hard from your end. Podcasters love a guest who does homework.

## Cross-platform adaptations

**Instagram feed:** post each clip as a Reel. Use the same captions but move hashtags to the first comment. Tag @thegraftpodcast and @bowelcanceruk in-frame.

**X/Threads/Bluesky:** thread the whole episode release as 6 tweets — hook, Part One recap, Part Two premise, Aperol Shitz moment, AI angle, link. Post the first clip as the quote-tweet of your own announcement.

**TikTok:** the Aperol Shitz clip will do absurdly well here. So will the "1 in 17 men" advocacy angle. Don't overthink the caption — 4 words max, let the clip carry it.

**WhatsApp / family + friends:** the day the episode drops, send the release-day post text to 15 people individually with a quick personal note ("hope you're well — round 2 went live today"). Three of them will reshare. That's three new audiences.

## Measuring what worked

After each phase, spend 5 minutes looking at:

- **Impressions** (did it reach?) — anything 3–5× your baseline is a signal to do more of that format.
- **Saves + shares** (did it stick?) — these matter more than likes; saves are the strongest LinkedIn ranking signal now.
- **DMs / comments from strangers** — the real win. "This helped my dad" beats any vanity metric.

If one clip outperforms the others 3:1, re-cut from the same conversation and post a second version of it a fortnight later. You're allowed to milk what works.
